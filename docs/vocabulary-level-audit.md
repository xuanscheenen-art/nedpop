# Vocabulary Level Audit

This pass keeps NedPop's daily word bubbles focused on learnable A0-A2 content instead of filling long word lists for their own sake.

## Reference Frame

- DUO/Inburgeren says A2 language exams cover speaking, listening, reading, and writing; writing includes short letters and forms.
- DUO/Inburgeren KNM themes include practical areas such as wonen, werk en inkomen, and daily Dutch society.
- NT2 TaalMenu A2 frames A2 practice around ordinary situations, short writing, forms, messages, grammar, words, and speaking in daily situations.
- Open KNM is useful as a secondary vocabulary sanity check for A2+B1 daily life and KNM terms, but it is not treated as an official source.

## Product Scope

NedPop currently sells:

- A0 free starter
- A1 life foundation
- A2 practical life task package

It does not sell guaranteed exam coaching yet. A2 therefore keeps practical task words for:

- GP / pharmacy
- gemeente
- forms and documents
- housing and repairs
- sick leave and basic work communication
- transport delays
- bills and insurance
- email and phone expressions

## Changes

- A1 daily word bubbles: 45 days, 450 active words.
- A2 daily word bubbles: 42 days, 430 active practical words plus 70 recognition words.
- B1 candidates: 263 words moved out of A1/A2 daily packs.

## B1 Candidate Policy

Words are moved to B1 candidates when they are too specialized, too formal, or outside the current A2 practical-life package. Examples:

- detailed tax and benefits terminology
- energy contracts and variable tariffs
- legal/safety administration
- job search and employment-contract vocabulary
- advanced public-health and care vocabulary
- formal text-analysis / connector vocabulary
- complex housing, insurance, and workplace administration

The B1 candidates stay in `b1CandidateWordItems` for future product work, but they do not appear in A1/A2 daily packs.
