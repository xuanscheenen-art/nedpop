# Vocabulary Level Audit

This pass keeps NedPop's daily word bubbles focused on learnable A0-B1 content while allowing A1/A2/B1 to grow to realistic public-course theme sizes.

## Reference Frame

- DUO/Inburgeren says A2 language exams cover speaking, listening, reading, and writing; writing includes short letters and forms.
- DUO/Inburgeren KNM themes include practical areas such as wonen, werk en inkomen, and daily Dutch society.
- NT2 TaalMenu A2 frames A2 practice around ordinary situations, short writing, forms, messages, grammar, words, and speaking in daily situations.
- NT2 TaalMenu B1 frames B1 practice around longer texts, listening, writing e-mails/letters/opinions, grammar, inburgering themes, and B1 exam preparation.
- TaalCompleet B1 public method pages frame B1 around A2-to-B1 growth, Staatsexamen NT2 Programma I preparation, participation, work, functional task language, and themes such as identity, health, neighborhood, money, work, education, safety, society, and media.
- Nederlands in actie publicly describes an A2-to-B1 method with themes including identity, the Netherlands, health, work, relationships, travel, sustainability, education, language, money, and art.
- Open KNM is useful as a secondary vocabulary sanity check for A2+B1 daily life and KNM terms, but it is not treated as an official required-word source.

## Product Scope

NedPop currently sells:

- A0 free starter
- A1 life foundation
- A2 practical life task package
- B1 independent task package

B1 content is textbook-aligned and task-oriented, but it is still not described as a guaranteed official exam word list.

A2 keeps practical task words for:

- GP / pharmacy
- gemeente
- forms and documents
- housing and repairs
- sick leave and basic work communication
- transport delays
- bills and insurance
- email and phone expressions

B1 adds independent-task vocabulary for:

- self-expression and relationships
- health, neighborhood, money, work, and education
- travel, sustainability, media, culture, and art
- opinions, presentations, meetings, formal e-mails, and longer texts
- public-service, legal/safety, tax/benefit, and workplace/customer-service reading tasks

## Changes

- A1 daily word bubbles: 65 days, about 650 active daily-life words.
- A2 daily word bubbles: 60 days, about 580 active practical-task words plus 140 recognition words in the source pool.
- B1 daily word bubbles: 70 days, 878 textbook-aligned B1 word items in the source pool.
- A0+A1+A2 cumulative review/recognition pool now targets roughly 1500+ words instead of a fixed 450/500 split.
- B1 is now an active independent-task layer rather than only a candidate pool.

## B1 Boundary Policy

Words are moved or kept in B1 when they are too specialized, too formal, or outside the current A2 practical-life package. Examples:

- detailed tax and benefits terminology
- energy contracts and variable tariffs
- legal/safety administration
- job search and employment-contract vocabulary
- advanced public-health and care vocabulary
- formal text-analysis / connector vocabulary
- complex housing, insurance, and workplace administration

These words can appear in B1 daily packs when they fit public B1 textbook/course themes. They are not used to pad A1/A2 daily packs.
