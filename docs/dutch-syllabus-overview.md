# NedPop Dutch Syllabus Overview

这是 NedPop 为课程设计整理的实用学习大纲，不是官方考试教材。

This is a practical learning syllabus prepared by NedPop for course design. It is not official exam material.

## Purpose

This syllabus defines what learners should know at each level from A0 to B2. It is intended as a blueprint for generating original NedPop lessons later.

It does not copy textbook content, commercial exercises, scraped materials, or official exam questions.

## Course Method

NedPop lessons should continue to follow the core loop:

1. Decode sounds
2. Link words
3. Master rules
4. Speak in scenarios

The syllabus data is stored in `data/dutchSyllabus.ts` and typed by `types/syllabus.ts`.

## Levels

### A0 Starter

Goal: Start from zero. Learn Dutch sounds, greetings, identity phrases, and very basic sentence building.

Learners should be able to:

- Decode common Dutch letters and sound combinations.
- Greet someone and answer simple yes/no questions.
- Say their name, where they live, and where they come from.

Core focus:

- Pronunciation foundation: alphabet, long/short vowels, vowel combinations, g/ch, sch, -en.
- Tiny sentence patterns: `Ik ben ...`, `Ik heet ...`, `Ik woon in ...`, `Ik kom uit ...`.
- Basic grammar awareness: pronouns, `zijn`, simple word order, yes/no questions.

### A1 Foundation

Goal: Handle basic daily life topics with short, simple sentences.

Learners should be able to:

- Introduce themselves and talk about family, home, work, school, and daily routine.
- Ask for time, prices, and locations.
- Buy simple items and handle basic transport situations.

Core focus:

- Daily vocabulary: time, family, food, supermarket, transport, address, weather, work/school.
- Sentence patterns: `Ik heb ...`, `Ik ga naar ...`, `Ik wil ...`, `Waar is ...?`, `Hoeveel kost ...?`.
- Grammar: regular present tense, core verbs, V2, question order, de/het basics, plurals, `niet/geen`, basic prepositions.

### A2 Bridge

Goal: Handle practical Dutch situations and prepare for A2-style speaking, writing, reading, and listening tasks.

Learners should be able to:

- Make appointments and explain simple problems.
- Call in sick, handle train delays, ask about bills, and go to the municipality.
- Write short practical emails and fill in simple forms.

Core focus:

- Practical vocabulary: huisarts, apotheek, gemeente, woning, afspraak, verzekering, rekening, vertraging.
- Sentence patterns: `Ik wil graag een afspraak maken.`, `Kunt u mij helpen?`, `Ik heb een probleem met ...`.
- Grammar: modal verbs, perfect tense basics, separable verbs, `want/omdat`, time expressions, polite requests, email structure.

Exam relevance:

A2 should align with practical language skills used in integration-style tasks: speaking, writing, listening, reading, and basic Dutch society situations. It must not copy official exam questions.

### B1 Independent

Goal: Express opinions, explain problems, describe experiences, and handle work/study-related communication.

Learners should be able to:

- Give opinions with reasons and examples.
- Describe past experiences.
- Write complaint emails and job applications.
- Participate in simple meetings or discussions.

Core focus:

- Vocabulary: work, job application, education, health, society, opinions, complaints, formal email.
- Sentence patterns: `Ik vind dat ...`, `Volgens mij ...`, `Aan de ene kant ..., aan de andere kant ...`.
- Grammar: past tense, expanded perfect tense, subordinate clauses, relative clauses, comparatives, argument connectors, passive introduction.

Exam relevance:

B1 should align with Staatsexamen Nt2 Programma I-style skills: reading, listening, speaking, writing. It must not copy official exam content.

### B2 Advanced Bridge

Goal: Communicate clearly in study, professional, and complex social contexts.

Learners should be able to:

- Present a structured opinion.
- Summarize texts.
- Write formal emails, reports, and argument texts.
- Discuss social issues and describe data/trends.

Core focus:

- Vocabulary: professional communication, higher education, abstract topics, policy, society, data, debate, reports.
- Sentence patterns: `Uit onderzoek blijkt dat ...`, `Er is sprake van ...`, `Hoewel ..., ...`, `Mijn standpunt is dat ...`.
- Grammar: complex subordinate clauses, advanced connectors, passive constructions, nominalisation, indirect speech, formal register, argument structure.

Exam relevance:

B2 should align with Staatsexamen Nt2 Programma II-style skills: reading, listening, speaking, writing. It must not copy official exam content.

## Notes For Future Lesson Generation

Use the syllabus as a source of targets, not as lesson text.

For each generated NedPop lesson:

- Keep Dutch examples original and level-appropriate.
- Keep Chinese explanations beginner-friendly and memory-hook oriented.
- Keep English explanations concise and supportive.
- Use the syllabus to choose scope, not to copy external material.
- Convert syllabus items into the NedPop method loop: Decode → Link → Rule → Speak.

## Data Quality Checklist

Before turning syllabus content into lessons, check:

- Does the level match the learner's actual output ability?
- Are Dutch examples simple enough for the level?
- Are Chinese learner notes specific and useful?
- Is the task practical rather than textbook-like?
- Is the lesson original and free from copied textbook/exam content?
