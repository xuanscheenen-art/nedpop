# NedPop Special Forms Sources

这是 NedPop 为 A0-B1 学习者整理的荷兰语单词变形查表，按现在时特殊动词、不规则过去式、完成式/过去分词、可分动词、比较级/最高级分区。

This bank uses public online references as guidance for structure and form checking. NedPop rewrites examples, Chinese explanations, and memory hints in its own learner-friendly style. It does not copy textbook lessons or claim to be official exam material.

## Reference Sources

1. [NT2.nl](https://www.nt2.nl/) - Lijst onregelmatige werkwoorden  
   Used for checking irregular verbs, simple past forms, past participles, and common auxiliary hints.

2. [Taalthuis - Irregular verbs](https://www.taalthuis.com/verbs/irregular-verbs/)  
   Used for irregular verb form checking and learner notes around `zijn` with movement or direction.

3. [The Dutch Online Academy - Irregular perfectum](https://thedutchonlineacademy.com/en/grammar/irregular-perfectum/)  
   Used for choosing high-frequency irregular perfect tense items and keeping examples beginner-friendly.

4. [The Dutch Online Academy - Perfectum](https://thedutchonlineacademy.com/en/grammar/perfectum/)  
   Used for structuring the `hebben` vs `zijn` perfect tense explanation.

5. [Zichtbaar Nederlands - Scheidbare werkwoorden](https://zichtbaarnederlands.nl/nl/verbum/scheidbare_werkwoorden)  
   Used for separable verb structure: prefix behavior in present tense and participle behavior in perfect tense.

6. [NT2 TaalMenu A1](https://www.nt2taalmenu.nl/nt2-a1/)  
   Used for A1 vocabulary scope and deciding which beginner forms are worth prioritizing.

7. [TaalBoost](https://www.taalboost.nl/)  
   Used as a frequency/scope reference for practical 0-A2 verbs.

## Editorial Rules

- Cover each supported special-form pattern as a reference section.
- Keep entries organized by pattern and level so A0-B1 learners can quickly check the form they need.
- Prefer words learners will meet in courses, forms, phone calls, appointments, housing, travel, and basic work communication.
- Rewrite all examples in NedPop style.
- Mark `verplaatsen` as a useful contrast because it looks prefix-like but is not separable.
- Do not call this official exam material.
